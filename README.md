## 🌍 Executive Overview & Core Mission

While traditional domestic portals (such as the standard `ai-home.world` or PropertyFinder) focus on local UAE buyers and local brokers, **AI HOME GLOBAL** is engineered with a single objective:

> **To market UAE real estate exclusively to the rest of the world through overseas brokers and international wealth professionals.**

International agents in London, Munich, Frankfurt, New York, Singapore, Zurich, and Hong Kong face distinct challenges when selling Dubai properties:
1. **Fear of Client Circumvention**: Agents worry developers or local brokers will bypass them once client contact info is shared.
2. **Branding Friction**: Marketing decks usually feature developer contacts or local UAE agency hotlines.
3. **Currency & Tax Confusion**: Foreign clients think in EUR, USD, or GBP, and need comparisons against domestic tax rates (e.g. German *Grunderwerbsteuer*, *Einkommensteuer*, *Spekulationssteuer*).
4. **Licensing Barriers**: Foreign brokers do not hold a UAE DLD/RERA broker license.

**AI HOME GLOBAL solves all four barriers.**
