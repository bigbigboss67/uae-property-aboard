Privacy, RERA/DLD Escrow & Cross-Border Governance

1. Privacy Policy — EU/UK GDPR & UAE PDPL (Federal Decree-Law No. 45 of 2021)

Data Controller: AI Home Global Gateway operates a B2B cross-border real estate syndication and KYC verification platform.

Lawful Basis: Professional broker/agency directory profiles are processed under Legitimate Interests (GDPR Art. 6(1)(f)) for B2B real estate networking. Buyer KYC & Deal Room records are processed under Contractual Necessity (Art. 6(1)(b)) and Legal Obligation (AML/CFT Art. 6(1)(c)).

Blind-Mode PII Minimization: Buyer identities and direct contact details remain masked until all pre-contact fee locks and 4-party digital signatures are executed.

Retention & Rights: Deal & AML records are retained for 5 years per UAE AML law; directory subjects may request access, rectification, or erasure (opt-out) at any time via the Data Rights / Opt-Out tab (30-day SLA).

2. Terms of Service, RERA Bylaw No. 85 of 2006 & Non-Circumvention

Platform Role Separation: AI Home Global provides technology infrastructure, white-label presentation tools, KYC screening, and automated co-brokerage contracting. On-ground UAE brokerage and DLD/Oqood execution are performed exclusively by RERA-licensed UAE Brokerages (ORN/BRN holders).

Pre-Contact Fee Classification: Lock 1 ($350 Buyer KYC & Legal Audit) and Lock 2 ($500 UAE Agency Lead-Unlock Credit) are SaaS, verification, and marketing fees—never real estate purchase deposits.

RERA Form I & DIFC Enforcement: Broker-to-broker splits align with RERA Form I standards. Any 180-day circumvention triggers 200% liquidated damages enforceable before the DIFC Small Claims Tribunal (claims ≤ AED 500,000).

3. DLD Escrow Protection (Dubai Law No. 8 of 2007), Alternatives, Costs & Other Emirates

1. Is DLD Escrow the only way?

  • Off-Plan in Dubai: Yes — mandatory by law (Dubai Law No. 8 of 2007). 100% of reservation deposits, down payments, and installments must wire directly to the developer’s RERA-approved Project Escrow IBAN, verifiable in the official Dubai REST (Mashrooi) app.
  • Ready / Resale in Dubai: Developer project escrow is not used. Buyers settle via a DLD Registration Trustee Office Manager’s Cheque at Title Deed transfer, or through a Licensed Conveyancing / Private Escrow (DIFC / ADGM / UAE-regulated trustee) for remote or multi-step closings.

2. How much does a DLD Escrow account cost?

  • For Buyers & Overseas Agents: AED 0. Buyers do not open a DLD escrow account — you wire directly to the developer’s existing project escrow IBAN (only your normal bank SWIFT wire fee applies).
  • For Developers (Project Setup): Accredited UAE trustee banks typically charge AED 5,000 – AED 20,000 to establish a project escrow account, plus DLD/RERA registration and ongoing trustee milestone/audit fees.

3. What if you or the property are NOT in Dubai?

  • Remote Buyers (Outside UAE): Off-plan purchases close 100% remotely via SWIFT to the Dubai REST-verified escrow IBAN + digital SPA/Oqood. Ready/resale properties close remotely using a Dubai Courts e-Notary video Power of Attorney (POA) or UAE Embassy-attested POA with a DLD Registration Trustee.
  • Other Emirates: Each Emirate enforces its own mandatory escrow regulator — Abu Dhabi (ADREC / DARI portal, Law No. 3 of 2015 & Law No. 2 of 2025), Ras Al Khaimah (RAK Municipality / RERD, Law No. 10 of 2026), and Sharjah (SRERD, Law No. 5 of 2024).

AML / KYC Compliance: Under UAE Federal Decree-Law No. 20 of 2018, all overseas buyers undergo passport, sanctions/PEP, and source-of-funds verification prior to SPA issuance.
